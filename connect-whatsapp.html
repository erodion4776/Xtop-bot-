Important: Choose the right number

Before continuing, understand how your WhatsApp number will be used.

  • Use a spare or dedicated business WhatsApp number, not your main personal number.
  • Sabi will use this connected number to receive and respond to your customers automatically.
  • Depending on Meta's connection and migration options, normal WhatsApp mobile-app use may be affected.
  • Keep the SIM active and accessible because Meta may require verification.
  • Do not connect a number you cannot afford to dedicate to business automation.
Don't have a business WhatsApp number yet?
You may be able to register a new number during Meta's setup.
  • Select Nigeria (+234) when choosing your country.
  • Enter a mobile number you can access.
  • Complete the SMS or phone-call verification requested by Meta.
  • If the number is already registered on WhatsApp, follow Meta's migration or coexistence instructions where offered.
What about my personal WhatsApp?
Your personal WhatsApp is optional. It will not be connected to Sabi's automation. You can continue using it separately.

Having trouble opening the Meta registration page?

If Facebook opens a black screen, a blank page, or closes before registration finishes:

  1. Close the blank Facebook window and return to this original Sabi registration page.
  2. If you opened this page inside WhatsApp, use the Open in Chrome button above.
  3. Make sure you reopen the original registration link. Do not remove the registration token.
  4. Wait for the page to load completely before tapping Connect My Business WhatsApp.
  5. If Facebook keeps intercepting the link, check Android Settings → Apps → Facebook → Open by default. Review its supported-link settings and retry in Chrome. The exact options vary by phone.
  6. If the problem continues, send Sabi support a screenshot and the connection diagnostics shown on this page.

Never share your Facebook password or verification codes with anyone.

Having trouble opening Meta?

If Meta's page closes immediately on Android:
  1. Open your phone's Settings.
  2. Go to Apps, then Facebook.
  3. Open "Open by default" or "Set as default".
  4. If "Open supported links" is enabled, you can try turning it off.
  5. Return to Chrome and retry.

This is a troubleshooting option, not a requirement for every merchant.
