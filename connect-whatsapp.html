⚠️ Important: Choose the right number

Before continuing, please understand how your WhatsApp number will be used.

  • Use a spare or dedicated business WhatsApp number, not your main personal number.
  • Sabi will use this connected number to receive and respond to your customers automatically.
  • Depending on Meta's connection and migration options, you may no longer be able to use this number with the normal WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business mobile app in the usual way.
  • Keep the SIM active and accessible because Meta may require verification.
  • Do not connect a number you cannot afford to dedicate to your business automation.
