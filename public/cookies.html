Cookie Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026 · Last updated: 25 November 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how XTOP Retail Technologies ("XTOP", "we", "us" or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit Sabi by XTOP websites, marketplace, merchant dashboard and related online services.

In brief
Essential technologies support core functions such as account access, security and checkout. Optional functional, analytics and marketing technologies are controlled by your consent choices where consent is required.

1. What are cookies and similar technologies?

Cookies are small text files stored by a website in your browser. Similar technologies can include browser local storage, pixels, tags and scripts that remember choices or help measure website activity.

2. How Sabi uses them

  • Essential: support core website functions, account sessions, security, fraud prevention, navigation and checkout. These cannot be switched off through the preference panel because the service may not work properly without them.
  • Functional: remember optional preferences and improve convenience.
  • Analytics: help us understand how visitors use Sabi so we can improve performance and usability.
  • Marketing: may be used to measure campaigns or support relevant advertising where such tools are enabled.

3. Your choices

When the consent banner appears, you can select Accept all, Reject optional or Cookie settings. You can enable or disable optional categories individually and save your choices. Essential technologies remain active. Your choice is stored in your browser so we can remember it on later visits.

To change your choice later, use the Cookie settings link in the Sabi website footer. You can also clear your browser's site data, which will cause the banner to appear again.

4. Third-party services

Some services used to operate Sabi, such as authentication, payment, hosting, WhatsApp and embedded content providers, may use their own cookies or similar technologies when you interact with their services. Their use is governed by their own privacy and cookie notices. We aim to configure optional tracking technologies so they do not run before the required consent is recorded.

5. Browser controls

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies through their privacy settings. Blocking essential cookies or local storage may affect sign-in, saved preferences, payment or other website features.

6. Updates and contact

We may update this policy when our services, technologies or legal requirements change. The current version will be published on this page.
