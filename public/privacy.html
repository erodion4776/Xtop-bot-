Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026 · Last updated: 21 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how XTOP Retail Technologies ("XTOP", "we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, stores and shares information when you use Sabi by XTOP ("Sabi"), including our website, merchant dashboard, WhatsApp shopping and business messaging experience, and related services.

Who operates Sabi?
XTOP Retail Technologies, a business registered in Nigeria (Business Registration No. 9324817), operating from Ugbowo, Benin City, Edo State, Nigeria.

1. Information we collect

  • Merchant and account information: business name, owner or contact name, email address, telephone number, business location, login and account identifiers, and settings you provide.
  • Customer and order information: customer name, WhatsApp telephone number, delivery address, products, quantities, order notes, order status and customer-service conversations needed to fulfil an order.
  • WhatsApp and Meta information: WhatsApp Business Account and phone-number identifiers, business profile details, message content and delivery or interaction metadata made available when a merchant authorises the WhatsApp Business Platform connection.
  • Payment and transaction information: order totals, payment status, transaction references, settlement and subaccount details needed to reconcile payments. Payments are handled by Paystack. Sabi does not ask customers to send or store their full card number, PIN or bank password in WhatsApp chats.
  • Technical information: device, browser, log, security and diagnostic information generated when the website or dashboard is used.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts; connect authorised business WhatsApp accounts; display products; receive and manage orders; communicate order and service updates; process payment instructions and reconcile transactions; provide support; prevent fraud and abuse; maintain records; improve reliability; and comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. WhatsApp, Meta and merchant responsibilities

When a merchant connects a WhatsApp Business Account, Sabi processes the information made available through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform to provide the requested messaging and commerce features. Meta's own services and terms also apply. Merchants are responsible for having an appropriate lawful basis to provide their customers' information to Sabi and for giving customers any notices required by law. Merchants must use customer information only for legitimate business purposes, such as responding to enquiries and fulfilling orders.

4. When we share information

We share only information reasonably needed with service providers that help operate Sabi, including:

  • Meta / WhatsApp: to connect business accounts and send, receive or manage WhatsApp communications.
  • Paystack: to process payments, manage merchant subaccounts and settlements, and provide transaction records. Paystack handles sensitive payment credentials under its own terms and privacy notices.
  • Supabase: for database, authentication and application infrastructure.
  • Netlify: for website hosting and delivery.
  • Authorities or other parties: where required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a lawful business transfer.

We do not sell personal information to advertisers. We do not permit service providers to use information for purposes unrelated to providing their contracted services, subject to their own applicable legal obligations.

5. Storage, security and retention

Information is stored using our application and infrastructure providers, including Supabase. We use access controls and reasonable technical and organisational safeguards designed to protect information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. We retain information for as long as needed to provide Sabi, maintain merchant and transaction records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements and meet legal requirements. Backups and records required by law may remain for a limited period after a deletion request.

6. International processing

Our infrastructure or service providers may process information in countries outside Nigeria. Where this occurs, we take steps intended to ensure that the processing is protected in accordance with applicable data-protection requirements.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or object to or request restriction of certain processing. You may also disconnect a WhatsApp Business integration through the relevant Meta or business settings. Some records may need to be retained for legal, security or accounting reasons.

8. How to request deletion or contact us

To ask a question about privacy, access or correct information, or request deletion, contact Sabi through the official Sabi by XTOP WhatsApp conversation you use to access the service. Start your message with PRIVACY REQUEST or DATA DELETION and identify the relevant merchant account or customer/order details. Do not send passwords, card numbers, PINs or other secret credentials. We may need to verify your identity or authority before acting. See our Data Deletion Instructions.

9. Children

Sabi is a business and commerce service and is not designed for children to create merchant accounts. Merchants must not knowingly submit children's personal information unless they have an appropriate legal basis and any required permissions.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Sabi changes or legal requirements develop. We will publish the current version on this page and update the effective date. Material changes may also be communicated through the service where appropriate.
