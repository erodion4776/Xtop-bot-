Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: 21 November 2026 · Last updated: 21 November 2026

You can ask XTOP Retail Technologies to delete personal information associated with Sabi by XTOP. These instructions apply to merchant account information, customer information, and information received through a WhatsApp Business connection, subject to legal and security requirements.

To submit a request: Open the official Sabi by XTOP WhatsApp conversation and send DATA DELETION. Tell us whether you are a merchant or customer and identify the account or order involved. Do not send passwords, card numbers, PINs or bank login details.

What to include

  • Your name and the telephone number or email address associated with the Sabi account or customer order.
  • Your merchant business name, if you are a merchant.
  • The WhatsApp number connected to the relevant business account, if the request concerns a WhatsApp integration.
  • A short description of the information you want deleted.

Verification and processing

We may ask for reasonable information to verify your identity and confirm that you are authorised to make the request. We aim to acknowledge and process verified requests within 30 days, or within another period required by applicable law. If a request is complex or the law permits an extension, we will explain the reason and expected timing through the contact channel used for the request.

What may be deleted

Depending on the request and applicable law, we may delete or de-identify account profile information, contact details, connected-account identifiers, customer conversation data and other personal information that is no longer required for a legitimate purpose.

What may need to be retained

We may retain limited information where necessary to comply with law, maintain tax or accounting records, complete or reconcile payments, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, protect service security, or establish or defend legal claims. Retained information will be protected and used only for the relevant permitted purpose for as long as it is needed.

Disconnecting Meta / WhatsApp access

If you are a merchant, you can also remove or revoke Sabi's access from the relevant Meta Business or WhatsApp settings. Disconnecting the integration stops future access through that connection but does not automatically delete information already held by Sabi. Submit a separate DATA DELETION request if you also want us to review stored information.

Third-party records

Meta and Paystack may independently hold information under their own services and retention policies. A request to XTOP does not automatically delete information held by Meta, Paystack, your bank or other third parties. You may need to contact those providers directly for deletion from their systems.
